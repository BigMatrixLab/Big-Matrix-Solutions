Big-Matrix Research & Development Lab Research Projects
1. Multimodal Breast Cancer Intelligence Platform
Development of a comprehensive multimodal AI system integrating mammography, ultrasound, pathology, clinical records, radiology reports, and genomic data for breast cancer screening, diagnosis, prognosis, report generation, treatment planning, and clinical decision support.
2. AI-Powered Lung Cancer Detection and Reporting System
Development of multimodal AI models using chest CT scans, radiology reports, pathology findings, and clinical data for lung nodule detection, lung cancer classification, segmentation, staging, report generation, and treatment recommendation.
3. Cervical Cancer Foundation Model
Creation of large-scale multimodal foundation models using cervix radiotherapy CT, MRI, pathology, genomic, and clinical data for screening, diagnosis, contouring, treatment planning, and outcome prediction.
4. Automated Radiotherapy Contouring and Treatment Planning
Development of AI systems for automatic organ-at-risk and tumor contour segmentation, treatment planning assistance, dose prediction, and quality assurance for breast, cervix, lung, brain, and head-and-neck cancers.
5. Pathology Foundation Models for Digital Histopathology
Building vision and vision-language foundation models capable of analyzing whole-slide pathology images, identifying cancer subtypes, predicting biomarkers, grading tumors, and generating pathology reports.
6. Medical Vision-Language Models (Medical VLMs)
Development of domain-specific vision-language models capable of understanding medical images and generating clinically meaningful findings, impressions, recommendations, and diagnostic reports.
7. Medical Report Generation and Clinical Documentation Automation
Building AI systems that automatically generate radiology, pathology, and clinical reports from multimodal patient data while maintaining explainability and physician oversight.
8. AI Clinical Assistant for Healthcare Professionals
Development of physician-centered AI assistants that provide diagnostic suggestions, differential diagnoses, report drafting, treatment support, evidence retrieval, and workflow automation directly within hospital environments.
9. Early Disease and Abnormality Detection Platform
Research on AI systems capable of identifying subtle abnormalities at early stages across breast cancer, lung cancer, neurological disorders, cardiovascular diseases, and other chronic conditions.
10. Hospital AI Deployment and Clinical Validation Program
Deployment of AI solutions into real-world healthcare environments with clinician-specific user interfaces, workflow integration, prospective validation studies, safety monitoring, fairness analysis, and continuous performance evaluation.
11. Precision Oncology through Multimodal Learning
Integration of radiology, pathology, genomics, transcriptomics, clinical records, and longitudinal patient data to support personalized diagnosis, prognosis, treatment response prediction, and precision medicine.
12. Cancer Foundation Models for Low-Resource Healthcare Systems
Development of large-scale multimodal cancer foundation models trained on diverse datasets from South Asian populations to improve cancer care accessibility and diagnostic performance in resource-constrained environments.
13. Single-Cell Multiomics Cancer Analytics
Analysis of single-cell RNA sequencing, spatial transcriptomics, and multiomics data for cancer subtype discovery, biomarker identification, tumor microenvironment characterization, and therapeutic target discovery.
14. Brain Intelligence and Neuro-AI Platform
Development of multimodal AI systems utilizing brain CT, MRI, EEG, clinical records, and reports for neurological disease diagnosis, prognosis, and clinical decision support.
15. Cardiovascular AI Platform
Research on ECG, echocardiography, clinical records, and multimodal patient data for automated cardiac abnormality detection, risk prediction, and patient monitoring.
16. Human Movement Intelligence and AI Security Systems
Development of AI-based behavioral authentication systems that generate dynamic security credentials based on individual movement patterns and behavioral biometrics.
17. National Multimodal Medical Data Repository
Creation of large-scale, privacy-preserving multimodal healthcare datasets integrating imaging, pathology, genomics, physiological signals, reports, and electronic health records to support next-generation healthcare AI research.
18. Big-Matrix Medical Foundation Model Initiative
A long-term flagship project focused on developing Bangladesh's first large-scale multimodal medical foundation model capable of understanding radiology, pathology, genomics, clinical records, physiological signals, and natural language medical documentation within a unified architecture.
