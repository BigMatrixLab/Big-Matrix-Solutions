# Big-Matrix Solutions Joins the NVIDIA Inception Program

Big-Matrix Solutions is excited to announce its membership in the NVIDIA Inception program.

We build software for healthcare and business: AI diagnostics, hospital systems, SaaS platforms and e-commerce. More than ten of our systems are running in production, with five clinical partners and support around the clock. Much of the hardest work sits on the AI side, in medical imaging models that have to be accurate enough for a clinician to act on. That is the work this membership is meant to support.

## What the program is

In NVIDIA's own words: "The NVIDIA Inception program is designed to help startups accelerate innovation and growth. Program members get access to the latest developer resources and training, exclusive pricing on NVIDIA hardware and software, and exposure to the venture capital community. The program is free and available to tech startups of all stages."

Three things in that description matter to a company like ours:

| What members get | Where it matters for us |
|---|---|
| Developer resources and training | Our engineers train and ship imaging models: organ and tumour contouring, lung nodule segmentation, X-ray annotation. Current material from the platform vendor shortens the path from a research result to a dependable system. |
| Exclusive pricing on NVIDIA hardware and software | Training and validating medical imaging models is compute-heavy. Better pricing on the tools for that work lets us run more experiments and validate more carefully within the same budget. |
| Exposure to the venture capital community | Healthcare AI takes years of validation before it pays back. Introductions to investors who understand that timeline help us plan the next stage of growth. |

## How we plan to use it

**Faster, better-validated medical AI.** Products such as our [Auto Contouring Software](/project/auto-contouring) and [Lung CT Segmentation & Report Generation](/project/lung-ct-report-generation) depend on models that are trained, tested and retrained as clinical feedback comes in. Training resources and better access to tools mean shorter cycles between a clinician's feedback and an improved model.

**Skills that stay current.** AI tooling changes every few months. The program's developer resources and training give our team a steady way to keep up, rather than relearning each change on a live project.

**A clearer path to scale.** Hospital systems and AI diagnostics grow one deployment at a time. Access to the program's investor network helps us plan for more deployments without cutting corners on the ones we already support.

## What membership says about our work

It is worth being precise here, because credibility is built on accurate claims.

Membership in the NVIDIA Inception program is not a certification of our products, and it is not an endorsement by NVIDIA. Our products earn trust the same way they always have: through clinical partners who use them, systems that stay up, and results that hold up under review.

What membership does add is a direct line to NVIDIA's training, tools and network. For the hospitals and businesses we work with, that means the team building their systems learns from current material published by NVIDIA itself, not second-hand summaries. For partners and investors, it is one more signal, alongside the systems we already run in production, that our AI work is built to a professional standard.

## What comes next

Over the coming months we will share what the program changes in practice: which models we were able to train or validate faster, and what that meant for the clinicians using them.
