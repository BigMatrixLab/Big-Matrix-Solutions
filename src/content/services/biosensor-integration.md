# Real-time Biosensor & Wearable Integration

As the healthcare landscape shifts toward remote patient monitoring (RPM), the ability to ingest and analyze high-frequency data from biosensors is critical.

## Our Approach

We provide end-to-end integration solutions for:

1.  **Continuous Glucose Monitors (CGM)**
2.  **ECG & Cardiac Monitors**
3.  **Smart Pulse Oximeters**
4.  **Specialized Neural Sensors**

## Technical Capabilities

*   **Sub-50ms Latency**: Stream data directly to clinician dashboards.
*   **HIPAA Compliant Ingestion**: End-to-end encryption from device to cloud.
*   **AI Pre-processing**: Edge-based anomaly detection to reduce alert fatigue.

Our systems ensure that vital data moves securely from the patient's wrist or skin directly into the EMR, providing actionable insights in real-time.
