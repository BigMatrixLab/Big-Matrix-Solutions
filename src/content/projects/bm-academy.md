# BM-Academy: Transforming Education Through Technology

*Project Duration: 6 months | Completed: September 2024*

![BM-Academy Platform](/assets/images/projects/BM-Academy.png)

##  Project Overview

**BM-Academy** is a comprehensive Learning Management System (LMS) and EdTech platform designed to revolutionize digital education in Bangladesh and beyond. Built for scalability, accessibility, and engagement, BM-Academy serves students, educators, and institutions with a unified platform for course delivery, assessment, and collaboration.

### Technology Stack




Traditional education systems in Bangladesh faced critical barriers:

### 1. **Geographic Limitations** 🌍
- Rural students lack access to quality education
- Urban-rural educational divide widening
- Limited availability of specialized courses outside major cities

### 2. **Infrastructure Constraints** 🏫
- Insufficient classroom space in schools and universities
- High student-to-teacher ratios (40:1 average)
- Limited access to practical learning resources

### 3. **Technology Gaps** 💻
- Low internet penetration in rural areas (< 30%)
- Expensive data plans limit video consumption
- Most students access education via smartphones only

### 4. **Assessment Inefficiencies** 📝
- Manual grading delays feedback by weeks
- Limited analytics on student performance
- No personalized learning pathways

BM-Academy demonstrates that **education technology, designed with local context in mind, can democratize access to quality learning**. - **AI/ML**: GPT-4 for essay grading, custom recommendation engine
- **Video**: Adaptive bitrate streaming, H.265 encoding
