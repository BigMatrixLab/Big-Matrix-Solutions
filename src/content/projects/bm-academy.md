# BM-Academy

![BM-Academy Platform](/assets/images/projects/BM-Academy.png)

## Project Overview

BM-Academy is a growing learning platform focused on helping medical students, young doctors, and early-stage researchers transition into AI and computational research.

The main goal is to bridge the gap between medical knowledge and computer science, especially for those who want to apply AI in medical research but do not have a strong technical background.

Currently, the platform offers two structured courses, and one batch with 10+ students has been completed.

You can visit the platform here: [BM-Academy](https://academy.big-matrix.com/)

## Focus Area

- Medical students and doctors interested in AI and research  
- Beginners from non-CS backgrounds entering computational medicine  
- Practical learning focused on applying AI in healthcare research  

## Technology Stack

- React for the frontend interface  
- Django for backend APIs and business logic  
- MySQL for data storage

## Conclusion

BM-Academy is an ongoing initiative focused on making AI and computational research more accessible to medical students and young doctors. By simplifying the technical learning curve, it helps learners connect their domain knowledge with modern AI tools and research methods. The platform continues to evolve based on feedback from each batch, with an emphasis on practical learning and real-world application in medical research.
