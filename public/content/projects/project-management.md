### Technology Stack




Modern enterprises face critical project management challenges:

### 1. **Complexity Overload** 🌀
- Projects involve 10-50+ team members across departments
- Dependencies between tasks create bottlenecks
- Traditional tools (spreadsheets, basic PM software) can't scale
- Visibility gaps between management and execution teams

### 2. **Unrealistic Planning** ⏰
- 58% of projects exceed initial time estimates
- Manual effort estimation leads to chronic underestimation
- No historical data analysis for improved future planning
- Scope creep derails 45% of projects

### 3. **Communication Silos** 🗣️
- Information scattered across email, Slack, meetings
- Status updates require manual aggregation
- Stakeholders lack real-time visibility
- Critical updates buried in noise

### 4. **Resource Allocation** 👥
- Team members overcommitted or underutilized
- Skills mismatch leads to task reassignments
- No predictive capacity planning
- Burnout from unrealistic workloads
