## Case Study: National COVID-19 Response Platform

**Client**: Ministry of Health, Bangladesh  
**Challenge**: Track vaccination progress across 170M population  

**Solution Delivered**:
- Mobile app for vaccination sites (register, vaccinate, report)
- National dashboard (real-time vaccination coverage by district)
- SMS-based vaccine appointment system (handles 1M+ messages/day)
- Vaccine cold chain monitoring (temperature sensors at 5,000+ sites)
- Certificate generation and verification (QR codes)

**Results**:
- 80% population vaccinated in 18 months
- Zero vaccine wastage due to cold chain failures
- Reduced vaccine hesitancy through SMS campaigns
- Enabled international travel with verified certificates
